# Lidarr 3.2.1.5196-ai.26

The Library table now aligns **Rename Files** and **Retag** headers with their rows. Both columns use compact, theme-aware status text and icons. Click a status to open the artist's preview, or hover for details.

Load the Linux amd64 archive with `docker load -i lidarr-ai-3.2.1.5196-ai.26-amd64.tar.gz`, then recreate your container with `lidarr-ai:3.2.1.5196-ai.26` and your existing appdata, database and volume mappings.
