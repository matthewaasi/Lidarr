# Lidarr 3.2.1.5196-ai.21

Open **Unmapped Files** and select **Retry Automatic Import** to rerun automatic matching against artists already in your library. Use **Add missing** when you also want Lidarr to discover artists.

Load the attached Linux amd64 image with `docker load -i lidarr-ai-3.2.1.5196-ai.21-amd64.tar.gz`, then recreate your Lidarr container with `lidarr-ai:3.2.1.5196-ai.21` and your existing appdata, database and volume mappings.
