# Lidarr 3.2.1.5196-ai.29

The artist page now has **Search Unsatisfied** next to **Search Monitored**. It searches monitored releases of every type, including albums, EPs, and singles, when tracks are missing or files fall below the quality cutoff. Releases already in the download queue are skipped.

Load the Linux amd64 archive with `docker load -i lidarr-ai-3.2.1.5196-ai.29-amd64.tar.gz` and recreate your container with `lidarr-ai:3.2.1.5196-ai.29` and your existing appdata, database, and volume mappings.
